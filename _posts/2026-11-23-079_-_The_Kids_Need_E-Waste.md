Frank Palazzolo and Evan Allen join us to talk about reverse engineering (and keeping alive) the animatronics control system from the original Chuck E. Cheese Pizza Time Theatre. Jen met them at [VCF Midwest](https://vcfmw.org/), where they gave a talk on the project. You can [watch their VCF Midwest presentation on YouTube](https://youtu.be/9ZCycafB1u8). We talk about MAME, reel-to-reel tapes full of serial data, pneumatics, tracking down hardware that was sent back to corporate decades ago, and why the best reverse engineering tool is other people.
