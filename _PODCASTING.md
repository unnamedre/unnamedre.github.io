## Editing

### Setup
1. Create new audacity project
1. Drag all the audio files into audacity
1. Import the tracks twice (All effects are applied to one, the other is there in case we screw up)

### Sync Audio
1. Disable sync-lock (`Tracks->Sync-Lock Tracks (on/off)`)
1. If you have a combined skype recording, use that as the "time master" to slide the individual tracks to match it.
    - Mute all but two of the tracks and slide one around using the **Time Shift Tool(F5)** to line it up 
    - **Note that the remote recordings won't match exactly due to extra time delays from skype**
1. Once the tracks are sync-ed, re-enable sync-lock

### Add Intro/Outro
I usually put the cursor at time 0 and use Generate->Silence to get 30 seconds of silence at the beginning of the tracks. I then import the intro and outro wav files and place them accordingly. You'll need to disable sync-lock tracks again. 

### Exporting MP3
1. `File->Export->Export as Mp3`
1. Use the following settings:
    - **Filename:** episode\_xx.mp3 (Where xx is episode number with no leading zero)
    - **Bit Rate Mode:** Constant
    - **Quality:** 96kpbs
    - **Channel Mode"** Force Export to mono
1. Metadata:
    - **Artist Name:** J. Costillo and A. Prieto
    - **Track Title:** xxx - Tile (Where xxx is three digit show number with leading zero AND spaces around " - ")
    - **Album Title:** Unnamed RE Show
    - **Track Number:** (Show number without leading zero)
    - **Year:** current year
    - **Genre:** podcast

## Publishing

Episodes are hosted on Cloudflare R2 (`media.unnamedre.com`), and the podcast feed is `https://unnamedre.com/feed.xml`, generated by Jekyll from the posts. Libsyn is no longer used.

### Show Notes
Write the show notes in Markdown (e.g. export the Google Doc to Markdown). They become the post body, and the feed carries them to podcast apps.
- **DON'T FORGET the comments/suggestions and music attribution bits.**

### Upload and create the post
You need rclone and ffprobe, the R2 credentials in `~/.config/unnamedre/r2.env`, and the archive mounted at `/mnt/media/urep_archive`.

```
python3 _sitegen/new_episode.py <number> "<Title>" <episode.mp3> \
    --subtitle "<interview with ____, or a short summary>" \
    --notes <shownotes.md> [--date "YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM"] [--dry-run]
```

This copies the MP3 into the archive, uploads it to R2 as `episodes/unnamedre-NNN.mp3`, and writes `_posts/YYYY-MM-DD-NNN_-_Title.md` with the audio fields and a new guid.

### Publish
1. Review the post, and preview it with `bundle exec jekyll serve`.
1. Commit and push. GitHub Pages rebuilds the site and `feed.xml`, and the podcast apps pick it up from there.
1. **Never change an episode's `guid` once it's published**, or apps will treat it as a new episode.
